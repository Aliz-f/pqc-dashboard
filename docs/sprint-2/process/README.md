# Scrum process records — Sprint 1 to Sprint 2

Group 21 | PQC Security Monitoring Dashboard | Prepared 16 November 2026

These four documents consolidate the planning evidence and the continuing project process records. They draw on the Sprint 1 report and slides, the course project instructions and Sprint 2 change scenario, Jira MEM, GitHub main/v2, and the successful V2 tests.

1. **[Planning and transition](01-planning-and-transition.md)** — timeline, Sprint Goal, roles, estimated stories, capacity evidence, keep/change/defer, scope boundaries.
2. **[Risk register](02-risk-register.md)** — retained Sprint 1 concerns, new Sprint 2 risks, controls and open team decisions.
3. **[Decision log](03-decision-log.md)** — historical team-reported decisions separated from observed implementation choices and pending approvals.
4. **[Change / Product Owner feedback log](04-change-log.md)** — requirement timeline, proposed 2027 deadline, 2028 device dependency, story impacts and code-review corrections.

**What is not claimed:** A newly held planning meeting, an accepted contract amendment, a Product Owner's final approval, an agreed numerical risk matrix, or actual retrospective decisions. Those require evidence from the people involved. The documents instead distinguish what was recorded by the team, what is visible in Jira/code, and which decisions still need confirmation.
